# Presets

81 factory presets in 6 categories. Each preset is a full configuration: vocal style, tonal offsets, saturation character, compression macro, MIX AMOUNT, the effects section (parallel compression, exciter, reverb, delay, doubler, chorus, width) and, for the Vocal FX group and the backing stack, the voice stages (pitch correction, harmonies, octaves, band-limit, noise reduction, ad-lib role). Presets are applied completely: applying one resets the voice stages too, so nothing leaks from the previous preset.

You can also save your own sounds under **My Presets** in the browser (plain XML files in your user data folder).

## Artist names: what they mean (please read)
The artist presets are **named after the artists as a creative reference only**: a general tonal direction (bright or dark, dry or wide, dense or dynamic) based on common production tendencies. They are original settings. They are **not** the artists' studio chains, **not endorsed by or affiliated with** any artist, label or engineer, and they do **not** clone any voice. They have **not been validated by listening tests**: treat them as starting points and tune by ear. If a name is a problem for its owner, tell us and it gets renamed.

## Notes on the Vocal FX group
- **Heavy Autotune / Robotic Vocal**: retune speed 0 ms, humanize 0, chromatic (Robotic: minor pentatonic) correction. The effect is the point.
- **Natural Harmony / Backing Stack Ad-Libs / Octave Harmony**: harmonies follow the **KEY / SCALE** set in ADVANCED (AUTO MIX fills them in when it hears enough sung pitch). With the wrong key the harmonies are in the wrong key.
- **Telephone Vocal**: band-limit 450 Hz - 3.2 kHz plus hard saturation.
- **Whisper Vocal**: airy, close and heavily levelled (compression + parallel compression + exciter). It does **not** convert a normal voice into a whisper.

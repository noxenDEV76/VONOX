# Presets

72 factory presets (34 universal, 38 inspired by general production tendencies). Each preset is a full configuration: vocal style, tonal offsets, saturation character, compression macro, MIX AMOUNT, **and the effects section** (parallel compression, harmonic exciter, reverb type/amount/decay/damping, delay type/sync/amount/feedback, doubler, chorus, width). A unit test checks that no two presets share the same configuration.

## Disclaimer
The "US Rap-inspired" and "French Rap-inspired" presets use **descriptive names**. The artists below are listed only as *unofficial creative references* for a general tonal direction. These presets are original settings: they are **not** the artists' studio chains, they are **not endorsed** by or affiliated with any artist, and they do **not** clone anyone's voice. They have **not been validated by listening tests**: treat them as starting points.

## Not available yet
Presets that depend on modules that are not implemented yet are **not** shipped: Heavy Autotune, Robotic Vocal, Octave Harmony, Natural Harmony, Whisper Vocal, Telephone Vocal. They will appear when pitch correction, harmonizer/octave and a band-limit stage exist.
